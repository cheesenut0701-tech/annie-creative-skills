# Poster directions

## Monumental black-and-white theatre

Use near-black grounds, white monumental Chinese characters, cropped strokes, narrow rules, tiny metadata, and a subtle spotlight or metallic gradient. Best for theatre, music, and formal artistic recruitment. Mood: restrained, resonant, intelligent.

## Cyber-sport collision

Use black with electric purple, acid green, and white; diagonal type; athlete silhouettes; speed marks; rough brush fields; condensed side labels. Best for basketball, performance battles, or high-energy campus events.

## Editorial recruitment collage

Use a limited base palette plus event-specific accent, cutout participants, artifacts, tickets, screenshots, or show imagery. Let the title and collage overlap with controlled depth. Best for club recruitment and culture festivals.

## Botanical ink awakening

Use paper texture, lotus or botanical elements, ink calligraphy, generous white space, a small seal red, and asymmetrical vertical composition. Best for spring terms, traditional culture, reading, and reflective themes.

## Warm orange stage invitation

Use apricot/orange gradients, soft haze, elegant serif or calligraphic type, a delicate line frame, and one emblem. Best for friendly recruitment, vocal performance, and celebratory openings.

## Quiet reading artifact

Use aged beige paper, stains, archival numbering, handwritten Chinese, tiny serif metadata, and minimal brackets. Best for bookmarks, reading campaigns, and literary events.

## Identity mark

For logos or emblems, combine a simple geometric container with one music/club symbol, limited colors, and a form that survives at small size. Avoid poster-level detail.
