# Composition and typography

## Composition

- Start with a single dominant mass occupying roughly one-third to one-half of the page.
- Use controlled asymmetry. Counterweight a heavy title with a small emblem, date block, or isolated image.
- Let one element cross a boundary or crop off the page to create energy.
- Reserve a clear information zone for time, place, organizer, and QR code.
- Use negative space actively in ink, literary, and formal theatre directions; use denser layering only for collage and cyber directions.

## Type

- Theatre: monumental Chinese serif/sans pairing, compressed vertical type, or calligraphic contrast.
- Cyber/sport: condensed bold sans, italic movement, outline/shadow only when hierarchy remains clean.
- Editorial collage: one expressive display face plus a practical sans/serif system.
- Chinese cultural: calligraphy as the hero, modern Song/Hei type for information.
- Keep no more than two display personalities in one poster.

## Information priority

1. Event identity or title.
2. Emotional hook.
3. Date and venue.
4. Organizer, participation method, and QR code.

Do not invent missing facts. Use clearly labeled placeholders when necessary.
