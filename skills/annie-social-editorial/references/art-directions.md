# Art directions for social editorial work

Select based on subject, not habit.

## Black-silver performance

For music, theatre, formal recruitment, or an elevated event. Use black, smoke gray, chrome gradients, waveforms, spotlights, cursive accents, and high-contrast white type. Keep decoration sparse and cinematic.

## Playful campus recruitment

For clubs, departments, and student participation. Use hand-drawn frames, mascot-like figures, chunky outlined type, sticker modules, warm cream, candy pink, sky blue, yellow, and green. The system should feel welcoming rather than childish.

## Warm blue-yellow human story

For interviews, day-in-the-life stories, or countdowns. Use airy blue fields, sunrise yellow/orange circles, white birds, soft gradients, portrait cards, and music-related lines. Alternate illustrated and documentary sections.

## Retro ticket-board collage

For club culture festivals and game-like activities. Use kraft brown, ticket stubs, labels, barcode motifs, photocopy texture, torn paper, black condensed type, and small red/blue accents. Let modules resemble a notice board or archive.

## Acid green activist editorial

For environmental research or provocative campus issues. Use fluorescent green, black condensed display type, numbered findings, warning bursts, outlined illustrations, and photocopied evidence. Keep the message urgent and data-led.

## Chinese ink and research

For health, culture, traditional topics, or analytical reports. Use rice-paper white, ink black, mineral green or cinnabar red, vertical titles, calligraphic texture, botanical/archival imagery, charts, and restrained ornamental borders. Balance atmosphere with clear modern data presentation.

## Musical fan-zine gradient

For musical recommendations or themed screenings. Use deep brown/black grounds with warm amber, magenta, or gold gradients, circular show badges, theatrical serif type, small review cards, numbered recommendations, and a strong color-block ending.
