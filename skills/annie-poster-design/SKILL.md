# Annie Poster Design

Create one strong visual proposition per poster. Annie's poster style is diverse: the consistent trait is a decisive concept, bold hierarchy, and a memorable relationship between title, image, and mood—not a fixed palette.

## Process

1. Extract the event's emotional verb: echo, awaken, collide, bloom, gather, perform, or move.
2. Choose the most fitting direction from [references/poster-directions.md](references/poster-directions.md).
3. Select one hero device: monumental title, silhouette, emblem, botanical image, typographic crop, or collage cluster.
4. Place essential facts in a quiet utility zone separate from the hero composition.
5. Remove any element that does not strengthen the concept.

Read [references/composition-and-type.md](references/composition-and-type.md) before making the final layout.

## Quality gate

- One focal point is obvious from a distance.
- The art direction fits the event instead of repeating a personal color formula.
- Chinese display text is accurate and remains readable despite expressive composition.
- Logos, QR codes, dates, and locations have stable alignment and sufficient contrast.
- Texture supports the concept and does not imitate a generic template.
- The poster works both as a thumbnail and at intended print/display size.
