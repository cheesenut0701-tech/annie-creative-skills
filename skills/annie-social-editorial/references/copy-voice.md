# Copy voice

Use energetic, human Chinese with short titles and concrete verbs. Annie's strongest operational copy creates companionship: it sounds like someone inviting the reader into an experience.

- Hook with a question, invitation, or vivid situation.
- Prefer `一起`, `走进`, `听见`, `看看`, `加入`, `发现` over bureaucratic phrasing.
- Use short bilingual phrases only when they reinforce the theme.
- For research, keep claims sober and let numbers lead.
- For arts content, allow one poetic line, then return to clear information.
- End with a direct next step.

Avoid exaggerated slogans, long administrative paragraphs, and decorative English that is inaccurate or irrelevant.
