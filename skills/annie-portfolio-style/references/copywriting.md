# Copywriting voice

## Voice

Write like a visually minded creator speaking to another person, not like a corporate brochure. The tone is:

- warm and sincere;
- concise and image-led;
- enthusiastic without exaggerated claims;
- lightly playful, occasionally using a short exclamation;
- confident enough to name a taste or choice directly.

## Structure

Lead with a concrete preference or action, then add one human motive or emotional payoff.

Good pattern:

> 我喜欢把复杂的信息整理成清晰、好看、让人愿意继续读下去的画面。

For project descriptions, use a compact three-part sequence:

1. Context: what the project or audience needed.
2. Choice: the central visual/operational decision.
3. Result: the experience, response, or measurable effect.

Keep most labels noun-led and short: `运营作品`, `海报作品`, `摄影作品`, `书签设计`.

## Bilingual copy

- Chinese carries meaning; English adds editorial rhythm and international texture.
- Translate accurately rather than literally when a literal version sounds unnatural.
- Use title case consistently for English section names: `Personal Profile`, `Design Works`, `Photographic Works`.
- Proofread every decorative English phrase; visual text is still content.
- Do not repeat a generic English sentence across unrelated sections. Tailor the line to the section.

## Useful sentence shapes

- `热爱……，擅长……，也持续探索……`
- `从……出发，用……建立……`
- `把……拆成清晰的层级，让……更容易被看见。`
- `这组作品保留了……，同时加入……`
- `我关注的不只是好看，也包括……`

## Editing rules

- Prefer one vivid sentence to three abstract claims.
- Replace `负责了相关工作` with the specific action.
- Replace stacked adjectives with evidence.
- Keep introductions to roughly 40-90 Chinese characters unless the format requires more.
- Use slashes only for compact category labels, not as a substitute for full sentences.
- Preserve the creator's enthusiasm, but remove filler such as repeated `非常`, `优秀`, `高级`, or `热爱` when unsupported.

## Example rewrites

Generic:

> 我热爱设计行业，喜欢创造各种美丽和快乐的视觉作品。

More characteristic:

> 我喜欢把灵感做成清晰、鲜活、让人愿意多看一眼的视觉作品。

Generic project caption:

> 这是一个校园活动的运营长图。

More characteristic:

> 用轻快的蓝黄配色串起报名、流程与现场信息，让长图在信息密度高的同时仍然好读、好逛。
